# Nexa Finserv — Official Product Knowledge Base

**Internal reference: NF-KB-v1. This is DEMO data for a demonstration tenant. All
rates, limits and timelines below are illustrative and are not a real offer.**

Nexa Finserv Private Limited is a digital lending company headquartered in
Mohali, Punjab, serving salaried and self-employed customers across Punjab,
Haryana, Chandigarh, Himachal Pradesh and Delhi NCR. We offer personal loans,
home loans, business loans and loan against property. This document is the only
authorised source of product information for our AI assistant.

## Personal Loan — Salaried

The Nexa Salaried Personal Loan is an unsecured loan for people in full-time
employment. Loan amounts range from ₹50,000 to ₹25,00,000. Interest rates start
at 12.99% per annum reducing balance and go up to 24% per annum depending on the
applicant's credit profile, employer category and existing obligations. Tenure
options are 12, 24, 36, 48 and 60 months.

Eligibility for the salaried personal loan requires the applicant to be between
23 and 58 years old at the time of application, with a minimum net monthly salary
of ₹25,000 credited to a bank account, and at least 12 months of total work
experience with a minimum of 6 months at the current employer. A CIBIL score of
700 or above is required for standard pricing. Applicants scoring between 650 and
699 may still be approved at a higher rate at our discretion. We do not lend to
applicants with a CIBIL score below 650.

The processing fee on the salaried personal loan is 2% of the sanctioned amount
plus GST, subject to a minimum of ₹2,999. The processing fee is deducted from the
disbursed amount rather than charged upfront.

Documents required for a salaried personal loan are: PAN card, Aadhaar card, the
last three months' salary slips, the last six months' bank statement for the
salary account, and a recent passport-size photograph. Applicants who have
changed jobs in the last six months must also provide an offer letter or
appointment letter.

Typical turnaround for the salaried personal loan is a decision within 24 working
hours of complete document submission, and disbursement within 48 working hours
of sanction. Fully digital applications with Aadhaar-based verification are often
disbursed the same working day.

## Personal Loan — Self-Employed

The Nexa Self-Employed Personal Loan serves business owners and professionals.
Loan amounts range from ₹1,00,000 to ₹20,00,000. Interest rates start at 14.50%
per annum reducing balance. Tenure options are 12 to 60 months.

Eligibility requires the applicant to be between 25 and 60 years old, with a
minimum business vintage of 3 years and a minimum annual turnover of ₹15,00,000
evidenced by filed ITRs. A CIBIL score of 700 or above is required.

Documents required are: PAN card, Aadhaar card, the last two years' income tax
returns with computation of income, the last twelve months' current account bank
statement, GST registration certificate where applicable, and business
registration proof such as a Udyam certificate, shop and establishment licence or
partnership deed.

The processing fee for the self-employed personal loan is 2.5% of the sanctioned
amount plus GST, minimum ₹4,999.

## Home Loan

The Nexa Home Loan finances the purchase of ready or under-construction
residential property, plot purchase with construction, and balance transfer from
another lender. Loan amounts range from ₹5,00,000 to ₹2,00,00,000. Interest rates
start at 8.65% per annum floating for salaried applicants and 8.95% per annum for
self-employed applicants. Tenure options run up to 30 years or until the applicant
turns 65, whichever is earlier.

The maximum loan-to-value ratio is 90% for properties valued up to ₹30,00,000,
80% for properties valued between ₹30,00,001 and ₹75,00,000, and 75% for
properties valued above ₹75,00,000. The remaining amount must be funded by the
applicant as a down payment.

Eligibility requires a minimum net monthly income of ₹30,000 for salaried
applicants, a minimum age of 23 and a maximum age of 60 at application, and a
CIBIL score of 720 or above. Co-applicants are permitted and their income can be
clubbed to increase eligibility. A spouse, parent or adult child may be a
co-applicant.

The processing fee on the home loan is 0.5% of the sanctioned amount plus GST,
capped at ₹15,000. Legal and technical valuation charges of ₹5,500 plus GST apply
and are non-refundable once the valuation is carried out.

Documents required in addition to standard KYC are: the last six months' bank
statement, the last three months' salary slips or the last two years' ITRs, the
property agreement to sell or allotment letter, prior chain of title documents,
and an approved building plan where applicable.

Typical turnaround for a home loan is a conditional sanction within 3 working
days and final disbursement within 10 to 15 working days, subject to legal and
technical clearance of the property.

## Business Loan

The Nexa Business Loan is an unsecured working-capital loan for MSMEs. Loan
amounts range from ₹2,00,000 to ₹50,00,000. Interest rates start at 15.99% per
annum reducing balance. Tenure options are 12 to 48 months.

Eligibility requires a minimum business vintage of 3 years, minimum annual
turnover of ₹40,00,000, filed GST returns for the last four quarters, and a CIBIL
or CMR score meeting our internal cut-off. The business must be profitable in at
least two of the last three financial years.

The processing fee on the business loan is 2% of the sanctioned amount plus GST.

## Loan Against Property

The Nexa Loan Against Property is a secured loan against self-owned residential
or commercial property. Loan amounts range from ₹10,00,000 to ₹5,00,00,000.
Interest rates start at 10.25% per annum. Tenure runs up to 15 years. The maximum
loan-to-value ratio is 60% of the market value assessed by our empanelled valuer.

Properties that are agricultural land, under litigation, held on lease with less
than 20 years remaining, or lacking a clear registered title are not accepted as
collateral.

## Fees, Charges and Repayment

Prepayment of a floating-rate loan by an individual borrower from own funds
carries no foreclosure charge. Prepayment of a fixed-rate personal loan or
business loan carries a foreclosure charge of 4% of the outstanding principal if
foreclosed within the first 12 months, 3% between 13 and 24 months, and 2%
thereafter. Part-prepayment is permitted once per financial year after 6 EMIs
have been paid, up to 25% of the outstanding principal, with no charge on
floating-rate loans.

A bounced EMI attracts a cheque or mandate return charge of ₹590 including GST.
Late payment interest of 2% per month applies on the overdue instalment amount.
A duplicate statement of account or foreclosure letter is issued free once per
financial year and at ₹500 plus GST thereafter.

EMIs are collected by NACH auto-debit on the 5th of each month by default. The
debit date can be changed to the 10th or the 15th on written request, once during
the life of the loan.

## Application Process

The Nexa application journey has five stages. First, the customer shares basic
details and consents to a credit bureau check, which produces an indicative
eligibility figure in about two minutes and does not affect the credit score.
Second, the customer uploads documents through the app, the portal, or by
WhatsApp to our verified business number. Third, our credit team reviews the file
and may call to clarify income or obligations. Fourth, a sanction letter is
issued setting out the amount, rate, tenure and charges, which the customer
accepts digitally. Fifth, the NACH mandate is registered and the amount is
disbursed to the customer's bank account.

Customers can apply through the website, the mobile app, WhatsApp, by calling
1800-419-6272, or by visiting any branch.

## Common Questions

A customer asking whether checking eligibility affects their credit score should
be told that the indicative eligibility check is a soft enquiry and does not
affect the score. A hard enquiry is recorded only when a full application is
submitted after the sanction letter is accepted.

A customer asking whether they can apply with a low or no CIBIL score should be
told we require a minimum of 650, and that applicants with no credit history may
be considered for a smaller ticket size with a co-applicant.

A customer asking about a top-up on an existing Nexa loan should be told that
top-ups are available after 12 EMIs have been paid on time, subject to fresh
eligibility assessment.

A customer asking whether we charge anything before disbursement should be told
that Nexa Finserv never asks for any payment, deposit or fee before disbursement.
All charges are deducted from the disbursed amount. Anyone asking for an advance
payment in our name should be reported to care@nexafinserv.example immediately.

A customer asking to speak to a person should be connected to a human advisor
without argument.

## Boundaries for the AI Assistant

The assistant must not quote a rate, fee, limit or timeline that does not appear
in this document. The assistant must not promise approval, guarantee a specific
rate for a specific customer, or state that an application will definitely be
sanctioned; final terms always depend on credit assessment. The assistant must
not offer tax, investment or legal advice. The assistant must not collect card
numbers, CVV, OTPs, net-banking passwords or full bank account numbers in chat.
The assistant must not discuss another lender's products or compare Nexa's rates
against a named competitor's rates.
