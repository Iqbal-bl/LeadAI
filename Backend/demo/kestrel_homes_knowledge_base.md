## About Kestrel Homes

Kestrel Homes Pvt. Ltd. is a residential and commercial real-estate developer based in Mohali, Punjab. Kestrel Homes has been building since 2011 and has delivered nine projects and about 3,400 homes in and around Mohali, Zirakpur and New Chandigarh.

Kestrel Homes has three projects on sale today: Kestrel Heights (apartments in Sector 82, Mohali), Kestrel Enclave (residential plots in New Chandigarh) and Kestrel Arcade (shops and offices in Zirakpur).

The Kestrel Homes sales office is the Kestrel Experience Centre, Sector 82, JLPL Industrial Area, Mohali, Punjab. The Kestrel Experience Centre is open every day from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and on Sundays by appointment.

The Kestrel Homes customer care and sales phone number is +91 172 555 0142. Kestrel Homes customer care replies on WhatsApp Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 7:00 PM.

## What Is the Payment Plan?

### Payment plan for Kestrel Heights apartments

The Kestrel Heights payment plan for apartments under construction is a construction-linked payment plan. Under the Kestrel Heights construction-linked payment plan, 10 percent of the price is paid within 30 days of booking, then instalments are paid at construction milestones, and the final 5 percent is paid at offer of possession. The milestone schedule for Kestrel Heights is given in the cost sheet and the builder-buyer agreement.

### Payment plan for Kestrel Enclave plots

The Kestrel Enclave payment plan for plots is 25 percent within 30 days of booking and the balance 75 percent within 60 days of booking, followed by registry within 30 days of full payment.

### What is the booking amount at Kestrel Homes?

The Kestrel Homes booking amount is 2 lakh rupees for an apartment, plot or commercial unit. The Kestrel Homes booking amount reserves the unit for 15 days while the customer completes the application, and it is adjusted against the total price.

### Is the Kestrel Homes booking amount refundable?

If a customer cancels in writing within 7 days of paying the Kestrel Homes booking amount, the full booking amount is refunded. After 7 days, the refund terms follow the Kestrel Homes booking application form and the applicable RERA rules.

### How to pay Kestrel Homes safely

Kestrel Homes accepts payments only by cheque, demand draft, RTGS or NEFT to the project collection account in the name of the project. Kestrel Homes never asks a customer to pay into a personal account, a personal UPI ID or a wallet, and never asks for cash. Every Kestrel Homes payment is acknowledged with a printed receipt. Any request for payment to a personal account in the name of Kestrel Homes should be reported to Kestrel Homes customer care immediately.

## Home Loans

Kestrel Homes has tie-ups with several leading banks and housing finance companies for home loans. A home loan is sanctioned entirely by the bank, so the loan amount, interest rate and eligibility depend on the bank and on the customer's income and credit profile. Kestrel Homes does not quote or guarantee any bank's interest rate. A Kestrel Homes advisor can connect the customer to a bank representative who checks eligibility.

Documents banks typically ask for a home loan from a salaried customer: identity proof, address proof, PAN card, recent salary slips, bank statements for the last six months and Form 16. Documents banks typically ask from a self-employed customer: identity and address proof, PAN card, business proof, income-tax returns for the last two to three years and bank statements. The exact list is decided by the bank.

## Documents Needed to Book

To book a Kestrel Homes property the customer needs identity proof, address proof, PAN card, two passport-size photographs, and the booking amount. For joint ownership, the same documents are required for each co-owner. Non-resident Indian customers should see the next section.

## Non-Resident Indian (NRI) Customers

Non-resident Indians can buy residential and commercial property from Kestrel Homes. An NRI buyer needs a passport, a valid visa or OCI or PIO card, and a PAN card. NRI payments must come through the buyer's NRE or NRO bank account. A power of attorney can be used if an NRI buyer cannot be present for registration. Because the rules on funds and documents can change, a Kestrel Homes advisor confirms the current requirements for each NRI case.

## Possession, Delay and RERA

The possession dates given for Kestrel Heights and Kestrel Arcade are targets set in the project schedule. Every Kestrel Homes project is registered under RERA. The RERA registration number is printed on the Kestrel Homes brochure and on every payment receipt, and a Kestrel Homes sales advisor shares it on request.

### What is the difference between carpet area and super area at Kestrel Heights?

Carpet area is the usable floor area inside the walls. Super area is the carpet area plus a share of common areas such as lobbies, stairs and lifts. The sizes quoted for Kestrel Heights are super areas. The carpet area of a specific Kestrel Heights unit is stated in the cost sheet.

### Can I resell my Kestrel Homes unit later?
