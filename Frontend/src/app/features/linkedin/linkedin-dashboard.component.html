How Auto-Accept Works

  • Lead Creation: When a connection request is accepted, LeadAI automatically logs the contact as a Lead/Customer in your CRM.
  • Instant Welcome Notes: Engage your new connection immediately with custom personalized introductory notes.
  • Scheduled Automation: The system automatically runs background sync every ~2 hours throughout the day with natural jitter.
  • On-Demand Control: View, manually accept, or ignore pending invitations anytime in the Received Requests tab.
